About SQL Quest

  
SQL Quest is a free game for learning SQL. You play the new business analyst at Brightside Electronics, a small gadget shop, and answer questions from the managers by writing real SQL queries. The database runs inside your browser, so there is nothing to install.

  
There are 51 missions in 10 levels. Beginner levels cover SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY, GROUP BY, HAVING and JOINs. Intermediate levels cover subqueries, CASE, dates, WITH and window functions like RANK. Each mission shows a goal, a hint and the number of rows to expect, and mistakes are marked in the editor with a plain English explanation.

  
Made by Eternity Squared Tech for business analysis students.
